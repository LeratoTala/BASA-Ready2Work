# BASA: Ready2Work — User Validation Plan (Section 31.1)

## Overview & Methodology
Before scaling, BASA: Ready2Work must be grounded in direct feedback from young South African job seekers (aged 18–29 in township and peri-urban hubs) and local micro/small business employers (spazas, trade workshops, cafes, logistics, salon owners).

This validation plan tests our core value proposition: **Can verified informal skills and honest proof replace traditional CV gatekeeping?**

## 2. Interview Protocol & Questions

### Youth Job Seekers (Cohort A)
1. **Q1 (Current Frustration):** What is the hardest part about applying for entry-level work without a university degree or matric distinction?
2. **Q2 (Skill Passport Clarity):** When looking at the 4 skill labels (*Self-reported*, *Peer-endorsed*, *Task-assessed*, *Mentor-reviewed*), do you understand what each one means for your profile?
3. **Q3 (Evidence Upload):** How easy or difficult was it to attach photos or documents (trade certificates, supervisor letters) from your phone?
4. **Q4 (Privacy & Safety):** Do you feel safe knowing your exact address and phone number stay hidden until an employer shortlists you?
5. **Q5 (Recommendation Value):** Did the match score explanation help you see why a job was recommended to you?

### Small Business Employers (Cohort B)
1. **Q1 (Hiring Pain Point):** What is the biggest risk when hiring someone based on a standard two-page paper CV?
2. **Q2 (Evidence Trust):** Does seeing photos of completed work or a practical rubric score give you more confidence than references on a CV?
3. **Q3 (Shortlist Flow):** Is the candidate shortlisting flow clear, and does seeing the verification badge first save you time?
4. **Q4 (Payment & Payout):** Does confirming work completion directly in the app fit how you manage daily or weekly wages?
5. **Q5 (Fraud Safeguards):** What additional checks would you like to see before interviewing a candidate?
