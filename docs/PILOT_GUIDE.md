# BASA: Ready2Work Pilot Evaluation Plan

This guide helps the team test the product and present results honestly. It is a proposed evaluation plan, not evidence that a pilot has taken place.

## Before inviting participants

- Configure a dedicated Firebase development project and verify the deployment, security rules, App Check configuration and account-deletion process.
- Confirm the minimum-age and work eligibility policy, privacy contact, evidence-retention period and participant consent wording with the project owner.
- Recruit a small, opt-in group of candidates and employers through a trusted partner. Explain that BASA is being evaluated, not a guarantee of work.
- Use fictional accounts for public demos. Do not show real candidate contact details, private evidence or identifiable application data in slides.
- Agree on a short evaluation window and who will respond to safety, scam-reporting or deletion requests.

## Observe usability, not just feature completion

Ask participants to complete these tasks without coaching. Record where they hesitate, whether they finish, and what they expected to happen:

1. Candidate: create or complete a profile and add one practical skill with evidence.
2. Candidate: understand the verification label and who can view the evidence.
3. Candidate: find a suitable paid opportunity, understand the match explanation and apply.
4. Employer: create an opportunity with clear pay terms, review an applicant and shortlist or decline.
5. Candidate/employer: confirm work completion and explain what the work record does—and does not—confirm.

Ask brief follow-up questions: What was confusing? What did you trust or not trust? What information was missing? Would you use this again, and why? Capture feedback without collecting unnecessary sensitive personal information. Obtain permission before quoting or photographing participants.

## Report outcome metrics with definitions

The admin overview counts platform records. Treat these as activity indicators, not proof of social impact:

| Metric | Definition | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate profiles | Candidate profile documents available to the admin store | A profile is not necessarily an active or unique pilot participant. |
| Participating businesses | Distinct employer IDs on job records | A listing alone does not prove employer engagement. |
| Application records | Application documents currently visible in the store | Withdrawn or duplicate history is not a longitudinal funnel. |
| Currently shortlisted | Applications whose current status is `shortlisted` | Later status transitions replace this status; it is not a count of everyone ever shortlisted. |
| Completed work records | Work records marked completed | This records employer-confirmed completion, not independent verification. |
| Employer-reported payouts | Completed work records with `payoutConfirmed` | This is employer-reported and is not independently audited. |

Keep pilot counts separate from demo mode and seeded fictional records. For presentations, report the source, date range, denominator, consent basis and limitations. Never imply employment or payment was independently verified when it was not.

## Test matching responsibly

BASA's score is an explainable fit heuristic, not a probability of being hired. Ask candidates and employers whether the listed skills, evidence, availability and location reasons are accurate and useful. Review mismatches with human decision-makers before changing weights. Do not use the score to auto-reject applicants or claim predictive accuracy without a suitable, consented dataset and a measured evaluation.

## Suggested presentation

1. **Need:** Practical experience can be hard to show through a conventional CV.
2. **Approach:** BASA organizes skill claims and evidence into a Skill Passport with honest verification labels.
3. **Demonstration:** Show one candidate adding evidence, viewing an explainable opportunity match and applying; then show the employer reviewing that evidence.
4. **Evidence:** Present only dated, sourced pilot results and participant feedback. If the pilot has not happened, say so and show the evaluation plan instead.
5. **Trust:** Explain privacy, employer contact access, moderation and the limits of the match score.
6. **Next milestone:** State the specific integration, partner or pilot result the team will pursue next.
